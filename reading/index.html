CMCP Reading Group


“Without revolutionary theory there can be no revolutionary movement.”
-Vladimir Lenin


We host weekly reading groups every Sunday 5pm, reading theory and other leftist literature. Drop by to learn and discuss literature, and have fun! For location and more details, join our discord.

So far, we have read or are reading:

  • Socialist Reconstruction: A Better Future for the United States – Party for Socialism and Liberation
  • The Origin of the Family, Private Property and the State – Friedrich Engels
  • Value, Price, and Profit – Karl Marx
  • State and Revolution – Valdimir Lenin
  • Blackshirts and Reds – Michael Parenti
  • Wage, Labour, and Capital – Karl Marx
